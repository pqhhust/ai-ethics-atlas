## Use

Open the website. Notes, confidence ratings and progress remain in the current browser. Export notes to transfer them or keep a backup. No account or backend is required.

To run locally, serve this folder with `python -m http.server 8000`, then open `http://localhost:8000`. Opening `index.html` directly will not load the JSON reader reliably.

## Sources and scope

The Sources page identifies slide pages, broad recording intervals, and corrections. The project is based on *AI Ethics - Group Project.docx* and the course video guidelines. Proposed controls are recommendations, not claims that the bank implemented or tested them.

Legal text is sourced from official EU publications and French legal sources; each entry includes its original source URL and version. The legal appendix reproduces complete selected provisions. The compendium contains complete official instruments and judgments. French Labour Code articles are included in the appendix and linked to the official government labour-code service.

The consolidated AI Act is dated 27 July 2026. Legal application dates are explained by provision; a transition for some high-risk requirements is not presented as a delay of every AI Act duty. Lecture statements are distinguished from current legal wording.

The transcript-derived synthesis excludes raw classroom conversations and recordings. Private account details, API keys and group lists are not included.

Course slides remain attributed to their original authors. Legal texts and third-party course material retain their applicable rights; no blanket licence is asserted over them.
